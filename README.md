# Levita

Levita is a cool hologram cube with futuristic looks that floats the display on the glass pyramid..On Levita we can play music from spotify, play cool animations, gif, display time, display weather can many other cool stuff.

# What it is?
So basically a 20x20x20cm desk assistant cum Music Player but unlike other devices that directly displays on a screen but Levita floats the display on a glass pyramid with a premium look that makes it a futuristic and unique project.

# How it works?
The device uses a microcontroller that operates the whole thing and has a TFT LCD Touch display that casts all the animation, music playback, weather, clock and any other cool stuff..But but but...the display casts things from four direction ffrom different angles that all the sides seem to be the same...Then there is an acrylic pyramid placed on it that reflects the light from the screen and creates an illusion like the whole display is floating on air that looks wayyy cool...We will also use a speaker to play songs from sptify and even have an aux port to connect headphones or bigger speakers..

# Why it was made?
It was made so that anything that is generally cased on a normal screen can be displayed in this in air screen that gives a futuristic and premium look that look wayy cool..

# How to use it?
To use it..you need to connect the esp of Levita with your phone and then Levita is ready to use. You can play music, display stuff and do many more things..

# What are the required components?
The most important components for Levita are:

ESP32(Brain of the whole project)

SPI TFT 3.5" LCD Touch Module(The main display)

Acrylic sheets to create the floating display..

Note: For detailed knowledge about the required components refer to BOM/FINAL_LEVITA_BOM.csv
